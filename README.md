# Spotify Data Analysis
## Overview 
The goal of this project is to analyze how various factors 
relate to a song's and artist's popularity. Factors examined 
include artist genre, track runtime, album type, release month,
and historical trends for an artist. The insight gained from 
this analysis can be used to inform those working in the music industry 
on how to increase popularity of their music.

## Data Source
The dataset used in this analysis was sourced from kaggle and contains data for songs on Spotify. Features include artist, artist genre, runtime, album type, artist popularity, and track popularity. Popularity is assigned by Spotify on a 0-100 scale.

## Key Findings
- Pop has the most popular artists by a wide margin, followed by country and hip hop
- the most popular songs tend to keep track time close to 3.6 minute mark.
- songs released as part of an album have the highest rates of becoming popular
- Songs released in May have the highest chance of being popular
- The most popular artists have maintained an average song popularity of around 60 for several year

## Key Insights
This analysis can be used to inform decisions on how to maximize popularity.
Producing music in a genre such as pop, and keeping tracks close to 3-4 minutes in length
will greatly increase chances to become popular. Additionally, Releasing music as part of an album,
and during a month such as May where more songs tend to become popular will also 
increase the chances for music to become popular.
